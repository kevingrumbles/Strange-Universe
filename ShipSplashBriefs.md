# Ship splash art briefs

For an agent creating splash art, one picture per ship type. Each picture is a dynamic action shot of the ship swooping through space at speed, banking and trailing its engine glow, not a static portrait or a hangar view. The shared direction below sets the mood and camera; each ship section describes its shape and gives an **Action** line for how it moves.

## Shared direction (applies to every ship)

- **Mood:** grim, worn, utilitarian. This is a dark, industrial, used-future setting, not clean sci-fi. Hulls are scuffed, scorched and patched.
- **Palette:** muted and dark. Greys, gunmetal, olive, dull brown, with a single restrained accent colour per ship (given below). Avoid bright, saturated or glossy colours. Never pure black; the hulls must read against deep-space backgrounds.
- **Lighting:** one hard key light from a nearby star, with deep shadow on the far side and a faint cool rim light. Engine glows are the only warm, bright elements (orange to amber). Cabin windows glow dull amber.
- **Surface detail:** raised armour plates, panel seams, recessed vent grilles, ribbed panels, rivets and scorch marks. Nothing is smooth or polished.
- **Weapons:** keep them small and integrated. No long visible gun barrels except where stated.
- **Wings:** wings are not important in space. Where a ship has them, they are small and swept for looks only.
- **Composition:** an action shot of the ship swooping through space, never a static portrait. Use a dramatic low or three-quarter camera angle, so the ship is foreshortened and banking hard on a diagonal across the frame, with its nose toward the viewer's side of the picture and part of the ship passing close to the camera. Show motion: a bright engine trail, a faint heat shimmer behind the nozzles, streaking stars or dust, and slight motion blur on the far background but not on the ship. Give the ship a clear sense of speed and direction, scaled to its character (see each brief's **Action** line).
- **Setting:** deep space with a few streaking stars, and optionally a dark planet limb, an asteroid, or debris whizzing past to give a sense of speed and scale. The star's hard light rakes across the hull as it banks, and the engine glow lights the hull from behind.
- **Scale:** these are small, hand-built, working ships. No people, no logos, no text.

Colours below are the ship's RGB values in the game: **hull** is the main colour, **accent** is the trim.

## Shuttle: starter ship

- **Role:** the first ship the player owns. Slow, light, cheap, utilitarian.
- **Silhouette:** a stubby, blocky brick with only a slightly tapered nose. It is almost rectangular, built from boxy armoured sections of slightly different widths, like bolted-together modules.
- **Details:** one pair of short, stubby, nearly straight wings. A row of about ten small amber cabin windows along each side. Two small engine pods at the rear. A dark glass cockpit canopy near the nose.
- **Colours:** hull dull concrete-grey (92, 90, 86); accent dirty ochre (138, 92, 34) on the wing tips and a few stripes.
- **Feel:** a beaten-up work shuttle that has done thousands of short hops. Honest, boring, reliable.
- **Action:** Plodding but determined: a gentle bank with its two small engines burning steadily, a modest trail, a sense of effort.

## Runner: fast courier-fighter

- **Role:** fast and well armed for its price. Sleek, quick, a little stealthy.
- **Silhouette:** a slim dart or needle. A very long, narrow fuselage ending in a razor-sharp nose, with two tiny swept-back fins at the tail. No wide wings. It should look fast even when standing still.
- **Details:** a single large engine at the back. No cabin windows; only a narrow dark cockpit canopy set just behind the nose. Clean, minimal panel lines.
- **Colours:** hull cool slate blue-grey (78, 86, 102); accent dull blood red (140, 36, 40) as a thin stripe along the spine and on the fin edges.
- **Feel:** stealthy and predatory, closer to a dart than a jet.
- **Action:** A knife-edge diagonal dive at top speed, long and stretched by speed, a single hot streak of engine fire and heavy streaking stars.

## Hauler: light cargo ship

- **Role:** slow, boxy, a small trader's workhorse with room for extra cargo and little to defend itself.
- **Silhouette:** a fat, extremely boxy block with a blunt, almost flat nose. No wings at all. It is the widest of the small ships and looks like a flying shipping container with engines on the back.
- **Details:** heavy ribbed and plated hull sections of different widths. About four small amber windows. Two engine pods. Cargo-pod seams and hatches.
- **Colours:** hull muddy brown-grey (84, 80, 70); accent mustard (150, 108, 40) on hatches and stripes.
- **Feel:** a tired trader's truck. Hauled more than it should have.
- **Action:** A heavy, wallowing turn, engines straining, with a thick wide exhaust trail and a few loose bits of debris shaken off.

## Freighter: bulk carrier (a big ship)

- **Role:** huge, slow bulk carrier with thick plating and lots of cargo space. It barely turns.
- **Silhouette:** a long, thin central spine in three parts:
  1. **Bridge block at the front:** a wide, flat, rectangular block as wide as both rows of containers together, with a band of dark windows across its front.
  2. **Containers in the middle:** two rows of identical rectangular cargo containers, four per side, lashed along the spine. Some containers are in the accent colour, the rest in hull grey. Ribbed sides and raised frames.
  3. **Engine block at the back:** a wide block carrying a bank of four engine nozzles side by side.
- **Details:** no wings, no cabin windows along the side. It should look modular and industrial, like a train of boxes.
- **Colours:** hull dark olive-grey (66, 74, 68); accent faded yellow-ochre (150, 120, 44) on some containers.
- **Feel:** vast and plodding. The scale should come from the number of repeated parts.
- **Action:** A slow, majestic pass close to the camera, so the row of containers sweeps across the frame, the four engines casting a wide glow. Speed is shown by scale, not blur.

## Courier: small fast trader

- **Role:** a small, quick, thrifty ship for carrying valuable cargo a long way. Light weapons and armour.
- **Silhouette:** a short, rounded, slightly pointed body, like a plump teardrop, with a big rounded cargo pod fixed along each side. No wings. From above it reads as a central body flanked by two fat pods.
- **Details:** the pods carry a stripe in the accent colour. Two engines at the back, two small amber windows near the front.
- **Colours:** hull cool grey-blue (88, 98, 104); accent muted teal (56, 112, 118) on the pods.
- **Feel:** a neat, careful little courier that is carrying something valuable. The cleanest-looking ship of the lot, but still weathered.
- **Action:** A smooth, graceful banking curve, with both pods trailing thin twin streams, tidy and efficient.

## Interceptor: tiny light fighter

- **Role:** tiny, very fast and agile, with two guns and almost no armour.
- **Silhouette:** a twin-boom design: two slim, needle-like hulls side by side, each ending in an engine, joined by a short cross-piece at the back and a small central cockpit pod between them at the front. No wings. The gap between the booms is a defining feature.
- **Details:** sharp noses on both booms. An engine at the tail of each boom. A small dark canopy on the central pod. Because the ship is tiny, show it small in the frame with plenty of empty space.
- **Colours:** hull neutral dark grey (96, 92, 100); accent burnt orange (150, 62, 22) on the nose tips and the cross-piece.
- **Feel:** an insect, fast and nervous. A pest that arrives in groups.
- **Action:** A tight, aggressive roll mid-strafe, twin engine trails spiralling around each other, as if closing on a target, with muzzle flashes optional.

## Frigate: warship (mid-size)

- **Role:** a sturdy warship with three weapon mounts, a strong shield and heavy plating.
- **Silhouette:** a solid, broad, moderately blocky hull with a tapered but not sharp nose, and one pair of medium swept wings carrying gun mounts. Heavier and more squared-off than the Runner, much smaller than the Dreadnought. It should read as a fighting ship at first glance.
- **Details:** thick armour plates, recessed vent grilles, about five dim amber windows. Three engines at the back. Visible gun mounts on the wings, short and heavy.
- **Colours:** hull dark navy-slate (70, 76, 92); accent deep red (130, 30, 32) on wing edges and plating stripes.
- **Feel:** a military patrol ship. It has seen fighting, and the scorch marks show it.
- **Action:** A hard-banking attack run, three engines blazing, the wing guns firing, with tracer fire or return fire passing close.

## Dreadnought: capital gun platform

- **Role:** the largest ship: heaviest weapons, shields and armour, and the slowest turn.
- **Silhouette:** a hammerhead shark seen from above. A broad, thick, armoured crossbar sits across the front, bowed back toward the tips, thick in the middle and tapering to points. Behind it a blocky central body runs back to a block of four large engines. The crossbar is far wider than the body. It must look dangerous.
- **Details:**
  - A glowing red "eye" near each tip of the crossbar, like the eyes of a hammerhead shark.
  - The crossbar tips and front edge are trimmed in the accent colour.
  - Heavy armour plates and ribs run across the crossbar and along the spine.
  - Several rows of dim amber windows along the central body.
  - **No visible gun barrels.** The weapons are implied by armour and mounts, not shown as long tubes.
  - No wings.
- **Colours:** hull cold dark steel-grey (66, 70, 82); accent dried-blood red (112, 26, 30); eyes bright red.
- **Feel:** a predator and a fortress. The biggest and darkest ship in the game, and the one other ships are afraid of.
- **Action:** A slow, ominous advance straight toward the camera, so the hammerhead fills the frame and the red eyes glare, four engine glows behind it, other ships tiny in the distance.
